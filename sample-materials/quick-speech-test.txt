QUICK CLASSROOM SPEECH TEST — BEGINNER CANTONESE
Short source material for checking upload, generation, listening, and speaking.

Goal: Greet a classmate and say whether a fictional character is a student or teacher.
你好 | nei5 hou2 | Hello
早晨 | zou2 san4 | Good morning
再見 | zoi3 gin3 | Goodbye
我 | ngo5 | I / me
你 | nei5 | You
係 | hai6 | To be / yes
學生 | hok6 saang1 | Student
老師 | lou5 si1 | Teacher
我係學生 | ngo5 hai6 hok6 saang1 | I am a student
我係老師 | ngo5 hai6 lou5 si1 | I am a teacher

Grammar: 我係 + role means "I am ...".
Dialogue: 老師：早晨！
Dialogue: 學生：老師，早晨！我係學生。
Culture: Use 老師 to address a teacher, and 早晨 as a morning greeting.
Practice: Match 老師 with "Teacher" and 學生 with "Student".
Listening target: 你好。
Speaking target: 我係學生。
Roleplay: A fictional student greets a teacher and introduces their role.
