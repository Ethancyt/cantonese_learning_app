Slides 4–9

Digits and place value

  • 0 零 (lìhng), 1 一 (yāt), 2 二 (yih), 3 三 (sāam), 4 四 (sei), 5 五 (ńgh).
  • 6 六 (luhk), 7 七 (chāt), 8 八 (baat), 9 九 (gáu), 10 十 (sahp).
  • For 11–19, start with 十. 十二 (sahp yih) is 12.
  • Read from the largest place to the smallest. Say 零 once for a gap between nonzero digits.
  • Group digits in fours from the right. 百 (baak) is 100, 千 (chīn) is 1,000, 萬 (maahn) is 10,000, and 億 (yīk) is 100,000,000.

Try reading 47, 308, 30,030 and 1,050,000,000. Then invent a four-digit code and read it one digit at a time.

Slides 11–18 and 24

Shopping and prices

鋪頭 (poutáu) means shop. 買手機 (máaih sáu gēi) means buy a mobile phone. 貴 (gwai) means expensive, and 平 (pìhng) means cheap.

蚊 (mān) is a dollar, 毫 (hòuh) is 10 cents and 仙 (sīn) is 1 cent.

呢個手機幾多錢呀？

Phone numbers and practice codes

Say each digit in order. For the digit 2, say 二 (yih). Use a short fictional code in practice.

你個手機號碼係幾多呀？
Néih go sáu gēi houh máh haih géi dō a? · What is your mobile phone number?
我個手機號碼係一二三四。
Ngóh go sáu gēi houh máh haih yāt yih sāam sei. · My practice number is 1–2–3–4.
九二八六
Gáu yih baat luhk · 9–2–8–6

Slides 28–45 and 48

Eating out

The workshop introduced Cantonese dim sum, cha chaan teng dishes such as pineapple buns and milk tea, and street food in Hong Kong.

你好，請問幾多位？
Néih hóu, chíng mahn géi dō wái? · Hello, how many seats do you need?
唔該，可唔可以畀張餐牌我？
M̀h gōi, hó m̀h hó yíh béi jēung chāan páai ngóh? · Excuse me, could you give me a menu?
你有乜嘢推介？
Néih yáuh māt yéh tēui gaai? · What do you recommend?
我想要一碗雲吞麵。
Ngóh séung yiu yāt wún wàhn tān mihn. · I’d like a bowl of wonton noodles.
一杯凍奶茶，少甜少冰。
Yāt būi dung náaih chàh, síu tìhm síu bīng. · One iced milk tea, less sweet and with less ice.
係咁多，唔該！
Haih gám dō, m̀h gōi! · That’s all, thanks!
喺度食定拎走？
Hái douh sihk dihng līng jáu? · Dine in or take away?
埋單，唔該。
Màaih dāan, m̀h gōi. · The bill, please.
我要呢個。
Ngóh yiu nī go. · I’d like this one.

Order with number + classifier + item. Use 碟 (dihp) for a plate, 碗 (wún) for a bowl, 份 (fahn) for a portion, 個 (go) for individual items, 籠 (lùhng) for a steamer basket, 罐 (gun) for a can and 杯 (būi) for a cup.

Place special requests right after the item. 少冰 (síu bīng) means less ice, 走冰 (jáu bīng) means no ice, and 走青 (jáu chēng) means no scallions or coriander. Put 熱 (yiht) or 凍 (dung) before the drink name.
