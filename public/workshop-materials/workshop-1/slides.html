normally after the frame.

ASKING AND SAYING

 [lit: I’m a (nationality). What about you?]

Slide 21

— 你係邊度人？

我 + [country] + 嚟㗎。  ·  我係 + [country] + 人。
 ngóh + [country] + làih ga  ·  OR  ·  ngóh haih + [country] + yàhn
  I come from [country].  ·  I am [nationality].

We add  人 yàhn (person) after the name of a country to mean nationality.
e.g. South African in Cantonese = South Africa + 人 (yàhn)

nàahm fēi yàhn
南  ·  非  ·  人
South African person
